# Privacy Policy for Problematic

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Application Name:** Problematic  
**Developer:** Problematic Team  

Problematic ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure for the Problematic mobile application and associated backend services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When using Problematic, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:
- **Account Information:** Name, handle (username), and encrypted password credentials when you register.
- **User-Generated Content:** Problems, broadcasts, proposed solutions, comments, poll responses, and likes submitted voluntarily.
- **Device & Connection Information:** Non-identifying network connection state and logs strictly used for connection diagnostics and API synchronization.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
- Authenticate your access and maintain your user profile.
- Deliver problem broadcasting, community replies, and solution verification.
- Calculate community standing and Respectadeo (RD) scores.
- Send in-app community notifications when members propose solutions to your broadcasts.

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure
- **We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers.**
- Information is shared solely as required by law or to defend against fraud and system abuse.

### 4. Data Storage, Encryption & Security
- All network communications between the Problematic mobile client and our API server are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using standard bcrypt algorithms before being stored in the database.
- Local mobile caching is managed securely via sandboxed device storage.

### 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion
- Users maintain complete ownership of their data.
- To request complete account and data deletion, users may trigger in-app profile clearance or contact the developer at `support@problematic.app`.

### 6. Children's Privacy
Problematic does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this policy. Any changes will be posted within the application with an updated effective date.

### 8. Contact Us
For questions regarding this policy or data safety practices, contact:  
**Email:** `support@problematic.app`
