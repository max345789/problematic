Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · App: Problematic (com.problematic.app) · Developer: Sarath C

1. Overview & Commitment

Problematic ("we", "our", or "us") operates a reality-first social media community where people share authentic problems and receive constructive solutions. We believe privacy is fundamental. This Privacy Policy discloses our data collection, usage, and protection practices in compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy frameworks.

Zero Ad-Tracking Promise: We do not sell your personal data, track your location across the web, or embed advertising trackers.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver core community functionality:

  • Account Information: Display name, username handle (e.g., @username), and securely salted/hashed password credentials.
  • User-Submitted Content: Problems posted, descriptions, tags, reply discussions, solution markers, and direct messages sent voluntarily by you.
  • Device & Technical Information: Basic app version, operating system version (Android), and timestamp logs necessary for diagnostics and security.
  • App Preferences: Dark/Light theme mode preferences and Direct Message access settings (Everyone, Requests only, or Closed).

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Precise Physical Location: We do not request or track your precise GPS coordinates.
  • No Financial or Payment Data: Problematic contains no digital goods purchases or paywalls.
  • No Third-Party Ad Profiling: We do not partner with ad networks or data brokers.
  • No Contacts or Phonebook Scraping: We never scan your address book.

4. How We Use Information

Your information is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and display your user profile.
  • To publish problem broadcasts and display peer replies.
  • To compute community standing through the Respectadeo (RD) helpfulness formula.
  • To deliver 1-on-1 direct messaging when permitted by recipient controls.
  • To enforce community safety, eliminate spam, and prevent child exploitation.

5. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the Problematic mobile client and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Data stored on device is confined to the sandboxed application directory protected by Android operating system permissions.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have the right to request full erasure of your account, posts, comments, and personal information at any time.

To request account and data deletion:

  • Email: sarathme02@gmail.com
  • Subject: [DATA DELETION REQUEST]
  • Include your username handle. Your request will be fulfilled within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy (Under 13)

Problematic is strictly intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we take immediate steps to terminate the account and purge the data.
