Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Prevention & Safety Standards

Application: Problematic • Developer: Sarath C • Contact: sarathme02@gmail.com

Problematic is committed to ensuring a safe, supportive, and respectful environment for all users. We maintain a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) of any kind.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Any content, depiction, solicitation, grooming, or transmission involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children is strictly prohibited on Problematic. Any user who uploads, shares, transmits, or encourages such content will face:

  • Immediate and permanent account ban and hardware/IP identifier block.
  • Permanent removal and purging of all associated posts, comments, and messages.
  • Immediate reporting to national and international child protection authorities and law enforcement.

2. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Problematic provides in-app reporting tools allowing all users to flag any concerning post, message, or user profile directly. Reports are reviewed by human moderators with highest priority.

3. Mandatory Reporting to Authorities

In compliance with global laws including United States 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and regional child safety statutes, Problematic cooperates with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate law enforcement agencies to report any identified instances of CSAM or child exploitation.
