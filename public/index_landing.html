Social media forgot about real life

Most platforms reward performance. Problematic rewards help.

✕ Filtered reality

Feeds push everyone to show a polished, frictionless life, so real struggles stay hidden.

✕ Vanity metrics

Likes and follower counts reward clickbait and outrage instead of genuine human connection.

✕ Distraction and trolling

Serious personal or professional problems get buried by memes or trivialised in the comments.

Built for everyone in the conversation

A high-trust space centred on mutual support and tangible resolution.

For sufferers and seekers

A safe, stigma-free place to put raw thoughts, career hurdles, health challenges and personal pain into words, without judgment.

For problem solvers

Earn recognition for genuinely helping. Standing comes from verified help, not popularity contests.

For the network

An ad-free, transparent ecosystem where the goal is simple: problems get solved.

Respectadeo: reputation you can’t fake

Instead of arbitrary likes, Problematic introduces Respectadeo (RD), a reputation engine built on verified help.

RD = Σ ( Validated Solutions × Wquality ) − Penalties

Earned standing

Points arrive only when the post’s author verifies your advice actually helped.

Milestone levels

Levels unlock community trust badges and priority display in problem feeds.

Anti-gaming protection

Rapid-fire upvoting or self-collusion triggers automated reputation locks.

Safety is not optional

A dedicated moderator account and zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Moderation tools

  • Instant post removal with audit logs
  • Deleted comments marked with red moderation banners
  • Solution disqualification
