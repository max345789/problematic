Privacy Policy for Problematic

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 • Application: Problematic • Developer: Problematic Team

Problematic ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure for the Problematic mobile application and associated backend services.

1. Information We Collect

When using Problematic, we collect only the information necessary to deliver core application functionality:

  • Account Credentials: Username (handle), display name, and securely salted/hashed passwords required for authentication.
  • User-Generated Content: Problems broadcasted, solutions submitted, comments, votes, poll responses, and direct messages voluntarily exchanged with other users.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Non-identifying network connection state strictly used to manage real-time online/offline synchronization and API connectivity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate user sessions and maintain your user profile.
  • Facilitate global problem broadcasting and community-driven solutions.
  • Compute community standing and Respectadeo (RD) scores based on validated solution helpfulness.
  • Deliver real-time in-app notifications when solutions or comments are posted to your problems.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers. Data is disclosed strictly when required by law or to defend against malicious attacks, spam, and system abuse.

4. Data Storage, Encryption & Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our backend are encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Password Security: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using industry-standard bcrypt before storage.
  • Local Storage: On-device data is secured within sandboxed application storage and never accessible to other apps.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Users retain full ownership of their data. You can delete your cached session or request complete erasure of your account and associated broadcasts by contacting our developer support team at support@problematic.app. Upon request, all personal data is permanently expunged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Problematic does not target or knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Contact Us
